# Legal and compliance

**Not legal advice.** This is a checklist of questions to take to a UAE lawyer, tax adviser and data-protection adviser. The answers depend on your licence, free zone and customers.

## Questions to settle
1. **Entity and licence:** which entity contracts with customers; does the licensed activity cover software services and any payment-related role (the product records cash-on-delivery and settlement but should not hold customer funds without the right licence).
2. **Customs and freight:** the product helps a licensed broker prepare and hand over data; it does not file declarations. Confirm marketing wording does not suggest otherwise.
3. **E-invoicing and VAT:** the product generates invoice data and connects to accredited providers where configured; accreditation of the product itself is not claimed. Confirm obligations and timelines with the tax adviser.
4. **Data protection:** see security/DATA_PROTECTION.md. Confirm controller/processor roles, cross-border transfers, breach-notice duty and timing, retention periods.
5. **Customer contracts:** limitation of liability, service levels, warranty disclaimers for third-party connections, data return on exit, governing law and venue.
6. **Customer verification:** the verification flow collects trade licence documents and company identifiers; confirm the lawful basis and retention.
7. **Consumer messaging:** SMS/WhatsApp to end consumers needs consent and sender registration under the telecom rules; confirm requirements.
8. **Open banking and bank feeds:** read-only statement import by the customer's own credentials; confirm whether any regulated activity is triggered when the bank connection is offered as a service.
9. **Sanctions and fraud:** screening of customers at onboarding; the verification flow blocks duplicate identities but is not a sanctions screen.
10. **Intellectual property:** brand/trademark clearance (see `public/brand/approval-checklist.md`); open-source licence review of dependencies.
